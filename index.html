Questions owners ask

Is the pilot really free?

Yes. Founding pilots cost nothing and we won’t ask for payment details. In return, we ask for 15 minutes of honest feedback at the end and, only if it helped, a short quote we can share.

Do you need my passwords?

No. You sign in yourself while we guide you, and we don’t keep passwords or recovery codes.

What if we already got hacked?

Hisn isn’t an incident-response service. We’ll point you to an experienced provider straight away.

Who is behind Hisn?

Hisn is an early-stage, student-led project in Dubai. We’re running free pilots to prove the service works before we register the business and launch paid plans. You’ll always know exactly what we’re doing and why.

What do I keep at the end?

Your written 30-day action plan and the changes made in your own accounts. There’s no obligation to continue.
